# Non-Goals

The initial foundation intentionally avoids:

1. Depending on unfinished control-plane systems.
2. Requiring hosted AI services.
3. Requiring Bazel.
4. Requiring Kubernetes.
5. Requiring a database.
6. Requiring a production app.
7. Requiring a production service.
8. Requiring package publishing.
9. Requiring artifact signing.
10. Requiring advanced policy engines.

These may be added later through explicit ADRs and work packets.
